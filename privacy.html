NTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025 · 100% Zero-Telemetry Architecture

NTab is an open-source, minimalist new tab browser extension with smart multi-column bookmark management. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. NTab is built with an offline-first architecture with zero tracking.

1. Zero Data Collection & Tracking

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, track, store, or sell any personal data, search queries, browsing habits, IP addresses, or device identifiers.
  • No Analytics SDKs: NTab contains zero tracking SDKs, zero analytics trackers (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), and zero third-party advertising scripts.
  • 100% Client-Side Execution: All layouts, themes, calculations, and bookmark operations run strictly within your local browser sandbox.

2. Permissions Justification

Permission Purpose Data Destination
bookmarks Reads and organizes bookmarks into multi-column cards; performs user-initiated add/move/delete actions. 100% Local. Never leaves your computer.
storage Saves user preferences (theme, column width, font size, custom search engines, 7-day trash bin). Stored in Chrome's native storage.
topSites Renders the optional "Top Sites" widget. Local only via browser API.
sessions Renders recently closed tabs in the "Recently Closed" widget. Local only via browser API.
identity (Optional) Used strictly when you click "Sign in with Google" to backup settings to your own private Google Drive appDataFolder. Direct connection to official Google APIs. No intermediate server exists.
host_permissions (*://*/*) Used exclusively for client-side Bookmark Health Checker. Sends lightweight HEAD requests to detect 404/403 dead links. Direct peer-to-target request. Zero tracking or logs.

3. Cloud Backup Security

If you choose to enable Google Drive sync, your settings JSON is stored directly in your own private Google Drive AppData folder. The developers of NTab have zero access to your Google account, Drive files, or backups.
